The Warrior's
Codex

Built for Alessandro Guerrera

This is not an app. This is a system for building a warrior.

The Roman legionary did not train in a gym. He marched, climbed, swam, carried, and fought. His body was forged by purposeful movement. His mind was sharpened by discipline, art, and the study of those who came before him.

This codex is your daily operating system. It governs how you train, what you eat, what you watch, what you learn, and how you recover. Every element is designed to build you — physically, creatively, and culturally — into the person the world needs to see.
